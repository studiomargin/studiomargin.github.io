No. Use the free Google Sheets copy (one click after purchase) or the Excel file. Both work the same.

Set it up once on a laptop, then log appointments in the Google Sheets app on your phone.

I'm not good with spreadsheets.

You only type in the lilac cells. Everything else is protected and fills in by itself, and the Home page walks you through six steps.

Is it a subscription?

No. One payment, yours to keep, with free updates for every 2.x version.

Which should I choose?

Starter if you want pricing, appointments, clients and your monthly profit. Complete if you also want stock alerts, an income-goal planner, a tax-time year page, the Instagram price list and the example file.

Which currency does it use?

Pick from 25 currencies, or type your own code. Checkout is in USD.

Does it work for lashes, brows or hair?

Yes. Type any service with its minutes and price. The examples are for nails and makeup.
