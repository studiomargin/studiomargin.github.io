Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

StudioMargin sells the Nail & Makeup Profit Planner, a digital workbook. This page explains what information we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • When you buy: your name, email and order details, processed by our checkout provider (Gumroad). We never see or store card details.
  • When you email us or use the free price check: your email and the message or numbers you send.
  • On social media (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, X): public comments and messages you send to our accounts, so we can reply.

How we use it

Only to deliver your purchase, answer your questions, send product updates you asked for, and reply to comments and messages. We do not sell or share your information with anyone for advertising.

Social media automation

We use the official Meta and TikTok APIs only to publish our own posts and read comments on our own accounts. We do not collect data about other users.

Your rights and data deletion

To see, correct or delete any information we hold about you, email studiomargin.help@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data". We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.
